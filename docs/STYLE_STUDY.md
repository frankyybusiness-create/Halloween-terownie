# Halloween poster style study

What makes the reference posters work, written as rules we can reuse. Every Terownie template and generated asset should follow at least one of these patterns.

## 1. One ink on coloured paper (*Death by Disco*, *The Exorcist*, *Not Another Halloween Party*)
- **Two colours only**: one paper colour (cobalt, scarlet, mint) and one ink (near-black or a darker shade of the paper). Photos are turned into that single ink with hard thresholds and halftone, never full colour.
- **The grain is in the ink**: the black is full of paper-coloured specks, edges are rough, and the solid areas look patchy, as if under-inked. This one effect makes a clean layout look screen-printed.
- **Newspaper grid**: a thick outer border, then boxes divided by heavy rules. The headline sits in the top box, a photo box beside the facts box, and a full-width image band at the bottom.
- **Extra-condensed type stretched tall** (Anton or Bebas, squeezed horizontally) that fills its box edge to edge. Every block of text is justified to its box width; there's no loose text floating around.
- **One huge number** (price, date or "469") anchors the facts box. A small ★ glyph is the only ornament, used in date lines and lists.

## 2. A stamped red flyer (*Halloween Party 30.10*)
- Full-bleed scarlet, black condensed headline in the middle that fills about 90% of the width. Lines are stacked tight with no leading and slightly uneven.
- Pale four-pointed sparkle stars, distressed, scattered at mixed sizes. They're the only imagery.
- A hand-drawn ellipse circles the location, and small details sit in the corners like stamps (the date top right, 21+ bottom left, the URL at the bottom).
- Heavy photocopy grit over everything, including the type.

## 3. Cut-paper blocks (*House of Horror*, *Trick-or-Tear*)
- A flat orange paper background with **black blocks** cut slightly crooked. Lettering is punched out of the blocks in lilac or cream, in condensed hand-cut type where every letter is a bit uneven.
- The illustration is built from the same blocks: rows of black bricks, windows with green or orange glow, small jack-o'-lanterns.
- No gradients and no shadows. Everything is flat and handmade-looking.

## 4. A cinematic red monster (*Room & Rave*, *10.29*, our *Wake the Dead*)
- A blood-red, dark-centred background with a big pale moon. The hero is a **giant claw or hand gripping a haunted house**, and bats fly out of the moon.
- The title is extruded, cracked, or set in serif horror type at the bottom, with a big Roman numeral or date behind it.
- A scratched, aged-paper overlay and creased edges.

## 5. Halftone duotone (*Disco Devil*, *Hallowia*)
- Purple plus orange or red, with photos shown as orange dots on violet. One wide headline in heavy orange at the top, then "A HALLOWEEN POP-UP BY" with the logo lockup, then **red torn strips** for event facts.

## 6. Comic and Y2K collage (*pump.boyz*, *Casa Obake*, the retro movie grid)
- Busy and saturated: comic panels, black header bar, a yellow "DON'T MISS OUT" burst, drippy horror lettering in orange and red.
- Y2K extras in the Casa Obake style: chrome, a flip phone, an error window, Japanese captions, type running diagonally.

## Rules for every Terownie poster
1. **Limit the palette per poster**: 2 colours for patterns 1 and 3, at most 4 for the others.
2. **The type fills its box.** Headlines run edge to edge, with condensed faces stretched tall.
3. **Distress is the finish**: grain or knockout on the ink, never a clean vector look.
4. **One hero image** (a hand, a house, a moon or a mascot) carries the composition.
5. **The facts are boxed or on strips**, never loose. Stick to the real details only: PUMP.BOYZ × EVPHORIA · HOUSE PARTY — HALLOWEEN SPECIAL · OCTOBER 30, 2026 · 3 PM · TWIN-PEAKS MOUNTAIN, BRGY. DAPDAP, TAYABAS CITY · @TEROWNIE_CAFE. No made-up prices, menus, perks or age limits.
6. **The real Terownie logo, recoloured to the poster's ink** (cream, black, navy or white). Never retyped.
